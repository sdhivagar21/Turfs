About FLOODLIT

Built for the love of the game.

We create premium sports spaces where friends, teams, athletes and communities come together to play, compete and make memories that last longer than the final whistle.

It started with one floodlit pitch in Velachery and a group of office friends who couldn't find a decent 9 PM slot. Today FLOODLIT runs ten venues across Chennai, from rooftop 5s in T Nagar to sunset volleyball on ECR.

Every venue gets the same standard: proper turf, bright lights, clean changing rooms and staff who know the game.
